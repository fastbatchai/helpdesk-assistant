# Account Lockout

Your account locks automatically after 5 failed sign-in attempts within 15 minutes. This protects the company from anyone trying to guess passwords.

## What happens next

The lock lifts by itself after 30 minutes, with no action needed. Please wait rather than trying again, because further failed attempts restart the timer.

If you cannot wait, phone the help desk on extension 4357. The engineer will verify your identity using your employee number and your manager's name, then unlock the account.

## Why it keeps happening

Repeated lockouts soon after a password change usually come from a device still using the old password. Check these places:

- Outlook and Teams on your phone
- Your tablet or a second laptop
- The office Wi-Fi profile on your phone
- Saved sessions on a shared warehouse terminal

Sign out of each one, then sign back in with your new password. Also check that Caps Lock is off and that your keyboard layout is correct, especially if you travel between offices.

## If you did not cause it

If you get locked out without having tried to sign in, somebody else may be attempting to use your account. Treat this as a possible security incident:

1. Reset your password once the lock lifts, using password.northgate.example.
2. Report it to the help desk and give the time of the lockout.
3. Deny any MFA requests you did not start.

IT Security monitors for sign-in failures across many accounts from one address. When they see this pattern, they may block that address and contact the people affected.

For password rules, see the Password Reset Policy.
