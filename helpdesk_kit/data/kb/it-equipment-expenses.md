# Expenses for IT Equipment

Your laptop, standard accessories and any company phone are paid for centrally. This guide covers things you buy yourself and then claim back.

## What comes free from IT

Request these through the help-desk portal. You do not need to pay for them or file a claim:

- a laptop charger, including one spare for hybrid workers
- a standard headset
- a mouse and keyboard
- a docking station

## What you may claim

You can claim up to 150 euros per item for home-office equipment, with your manager's approval beforehand. Typical items are:

- a monitor
- a webcam
- a better headset, if your work involves frequent calls
- cables and adapters

The limit across all claims is 400 euros in any two-year period. Items costing more need approval from your department head.

## What you cannot claim

- Laptops, tablets and phones. These must come from IT.
- Home internet or mobile plans, unless your contract says otherwise.
- Software subscriptions. Use the Software Request Process.
- Printers and printer supplies for home.
- Furniture. Ask Facilities or HR about ergonomic equipment.

## How to claim

1. Get your manager's approval by email, before you buy.
2. Keep the itemised receipt. A card statement is not enough.
3. Submit the claim in the expenses system within 60 days of buying, attaching the receipt and approval.

Claims are paid with the next payroll run once approved. For the local currency, Finance uses the exchange rate on the purchase date. In Leeds, claims are made in pounds with a limit of 130 pounds per item.

## Who owns it

Equipment you claim belongs to the company. You keep it while you work here, and you may be asked to return it when you leave. For questions, contact finance-help@northgate.example.
