# Teams Meetings

Microsoft Teams is Northgate's approved tool for internal meetings and chat. It is pre-installed on all laptops, and you can add it to an enrolled phone.

## Scheduling

Create the meeting in Outlook using New Teams Meeting, or in the Teams calendar. Guests from outside Northgate wait in the lobby until someone admits them. Do not turn the lobby off for meetings where Confidential data will be discussed.

Meetings can have up to 300 people. For larger events, such as town halls, request a Webinar through the portal at least five business days ahead.

## Recording and transcripts

Tell attendees before you start a recording. Teams also shows a notice. Recordings save to the organiser's storage and are deleted automatically after 60 days. Do not record meetings where Restricted data or HR matters are discussed.

AI meeting assistants from outside vendors are not allowed. The approved transcript feature is the one built into Teams.

## Meeting rooms

Each meeting room has a Teams panel. Tap Join on the panel, or use the room's code from your calendar invitation. Book the room in Outlook by adding it as a location. Report room equipment faults by using the sticker on the panel to open a ticket.

## Audio and video trouble

Check these, in order:

1. Select the correct microphone and speaker under Settings, then Devices.
2. Make a test call from the same menu.
3. Disconnect from the VPN. Sending calls through the tunnel is the most common cause of poor quality.
4. Move closer to the Wi-Fi access point, or use a network cable.
5. Restart Teams.

## Joining other companies' meetings

Teams meetings from other companies open as normal, with your Northgate account shown as a guest. For Zoom meetings, install the Zoom client from Company Apps. Other meeting tools work through the browser.

If a whole meeting room or site loses Teams, phone the help desk.
