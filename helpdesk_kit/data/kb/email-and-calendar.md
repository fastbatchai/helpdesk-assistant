# Email and Calendar

Northgate uses Microsoft Outlook for email and calendars. Open it on your laptop, on your phone once enrolled, or in a browser at mail.northgate.example.

## Limits

- Mailbox size: 50 GB. You get a warning at 45 GB.
- Attachments: up to 25 MB. Share larger files with a link to a shared drive.
- Email is kept for seven years, then deleted automatically.
- Deleted items can be recovered for 14 days. See Backup and Restore.

## Shared mailboxes

Team addresses such as dispatch@ or finance@ are shared mailboxes. Your manager requests access through the portal. Shared mailboxes appear automatically in Outlook within about an hour.

## Calendar sharing and delegates

By default, colleagues can see when you are busy but not the details. To let an assistant manage your calendar, open File, then Delegate Access, in Outlook. Mark personal appointments as Private. Delegates cannot see them.

## Out of office

Set this in Outlook under File, then Automatic Replies. Write separate messages for inside and outside the company, and do not give travel details in the outside message.

## Sending to people outside Northgate

Outlook shows a yellow banner when a message goes outside the company. Check the recipient before you send. Messages that contain Confidential data must be encrypted using the Encrypt button, and Restricted data must not be sent by email.

## Common problems

- **Outlook keeps asking for a password:** this often follows a password change. Close Outlook and reopen it, or see Account Lockout if your account has locked.
- **Missing email:** look in Junk and Quarantine first.
- **Suspicious email:** do not click on anything. Use the Report Phish button.
- **Calendar out of sync on your phone:** remove the account and add it again.
