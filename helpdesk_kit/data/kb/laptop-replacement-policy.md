# Laptop Replacement Policy

Every employee gets one company laptop. Laptops are replaced on a four-year cycle, counted from the date of issue. IT Asset Management contacts you about two months before your replacement is due, so you do not need to request it.

## Standard models

- Standard: 14-inch laptop with 16 GB memory, for most roles
- Performance: 16-inch laptop with 32 GB memory, for engineering, analytics and design roles

Moving to the Performance model requires your manager's approval and a short business reason, given through the help-desk portal.

## Early replacement

You may get a replacement before four years if:

- the repair would cost more than 50% of the laptop's current value
- the laptop has failed twice for the same reason within 12 months
- your role changes and the current model cannot run the software you need

Open a ticket under Hardware and describe the fault. An engineer will assess it, usually within three business days. A temporary loan laptop is available while yours is assessed.

## Damage, loss and theft

Report damage through the portal. Report loss or theft by phone right away on extension 4357, because the laptop must be locked remotely. Theft must also be reported to the local police, and the reference number added to your ticket.

Accidental damage is covered. Repeated damage through carelessness can be referred to your manager.

## Returning your old laptop

You have 14 days to return your old laptop after receiving the new one. Move your files first. Files in your personal backup folder move automatically, but anything saved only on the desktop may not. Old laptops are wiped and recycled.

To buy accessories such as a monitor or a headset, see the IT Equipment Expenses guide.
