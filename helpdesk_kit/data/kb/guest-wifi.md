# Wi-Fi for Guests

Each Northgate office has two wireless networks:

- **NG-Staff**: for company laptops and enrolled phones, connected automatically
- **NG-Guest**: for visitors, internet access only

Guests must never be connected to NG-Staff, and staff should not share their passwords with visitors to get them online.

## Getting a guest online

Reception issues guest vouchers. A visitor gives their name and the name of the Northgate employee they are visiting. Reception then prints or texts a voucher code.

1. Connect to NG-Guest.
2. A sign-in page opens in the browser.
3. Enter the voucher code and accept the terms of use.

A standard voucher is valid for 24 hours and works on up to three devices.

## Longer stays

For visitors staying up to seven days, such as auditors or vendors on site, the employee hosting them can request a sponsored voucher through the help-desk portal. Allow one business day. Visitors who need longer than seven days, or access to internal systems, need a contractor account, which their manager requests through the Onboarding process.

## What guests can and cannot reach

NG-Guest reaches the public internet only. It has no route to shared drives, printers or internal sites, and heavy downloads are limited. Video calls work well.

## Staff devices

Staff may use NG-Guest for a personal phone during breaks. A personal phone enrolled under the Mobile Devices guide can use NG-Staff.

## Problems

If the sign-in page does not open, try visiting any plain web address in the browser. If a voucher is rejected, go back to reception for a new one. If NG-Guest is down for everyone, reception will contact the help desk.
