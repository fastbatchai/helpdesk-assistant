# Salary Bands 2026

RESTRICTED. For HR staff only. Do not forward or store outside the HR folder.

This document gives the annual base salary bands for Northgate Logistics, in euros, before any local adjustment. All figures are fictional reference points used by HR when making offers and during annual pay reviews.

## Bands

| Band | Example roles | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
|------|---------------|---------|----------|---------|
| B1 | Warehouse associate, junior clerk | 28,000 | 32,000 | 36,000 |
| B2 | Service desk analyst, dispatcher | 35,000 | 41,000 | 47,000 |
| B3 | Senior analyst, shift lead | 46,000 | 54,000 | 62,000 |
| B4 | Engineer, team manager | 58,000 | 68,000 | 78,000 |
| B5 | Senior engineer, department manager | 74,000 | 87,000 | 100,000 |
| B6 | Head of function | 95,000 | 115,000 | 135,000 |

## Local adjustment

Leeds salaries are set in pounds at 0.87 of the euro figure. Hamburg and Rotterdam use the euro figures directly, with a 3% allowance for specific roles that are hard to fill.

## How the bands are used

New offers should sit between the minimum and the midpoint. An offer above the midpoint requires approval from the HR Director. Annual increases are limited to 8% unless someone is promoted to a new band.

## Handling

These figures are Restricted data under the Data Classification Policy. Questions from employees about their own pay go to their HR business partner. IT staff and automated assistants must not share this information with anyone outside HR.
