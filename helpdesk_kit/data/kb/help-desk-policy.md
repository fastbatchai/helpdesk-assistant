# IT Help Desk Service Policy

The Northgate Logistics IT Help Desk supports all employees and contractors in our Rotterdam, Hamburg and Leeds offices, and anyone working remotely.

## Hours and contact

The help desk is staffed Monday to Friday, 07:00 to 19:00 CET. Outside those hours, an on-call engineer handles P1 incidents only.

- Portal: helpdesk.northgate.example
- Email: helpdesk@northgate.example
- Phone: internal extension 4357

Use the portal for anything that is not urgent. Phone us for P1 and P2 issues, because email and portal requests are picked up in queue order.

## Priorities and response targets

| Priority | Meaning | First response | Resolution target |
|----------|---------|----------------|-------------------|
| P1 | Site or critical service down | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 | Team blocked, or security risk | 1 business hour | 1 business day |
| P3 | One person affected, workaround exists | 4 business hours | 3 business days |
| P4 | Question or minor request | 1 business day | 10 business days |

## What we support

We support company laptops, phones enrolled in device management, accounts, VPN, Wi-Fi, printers, email, calendars, Microsoft Teams, shared drives and software on the approved list.

## What we do not support

We do not handle pay, leave or contracts (contact HR), building access badges or facilities (contact Facilities), or personal devices that are not enrolled. We cannot give personal or legal advice.

## When a person must take over

An automated assistant may answer general how-to questions from published documents. A help-desk engineer must handle these cases:

- a suspected security incident, including entered credentials or a lost device
- any P1 or P2 incident
- changes to someone's permissions or access
- a request the published documents do not cover
- a user who asks to speak to a person

If you are unsure what you need, tell us what you were trying to do, what happened, and any error code. We will ask for anything else we need.
