# IT Offboarding for Leavers

When someone leaves Northgate, their manager and IT work together to close accounts, recover equipment and keep important data.

## For managers

Open a Leaver request in the help-desk portal as soon as you know the leaving date, and at least five business days beforehand. Tell us:

- the last working day and time
- who should receive the person's files and mailbox
- what company equipment they have

If the departure is urgent or sensitive, phone the help desk instead of using the portal. We can disable an account within 30 minutes, and the request is visible only to the engineers handling it.

## For leavers

Before your last day:

- Move team documents to the correct shared drive.
- Hand over ownership of any shared folder, Teams channel or mailbox.
- Remove your personal files from your company laptop. You will not have access afterwards.
- Do not copy company data to personal storage. This is logged and may be investigated.

## On the last day

Hand your laptop, company phone, hardware security key and badge to your manager or local IT desk. Remote staff get a prepaid shipping box. Equipment must arrive within 14 days.

Your account is disabled at the end of your last working day. The work area on any personal phone is removed automatically, and your NorthKey entry stops working.

## After you leave

- Your mailbox stays available to your manager for 30 days, along with an automatic reply if requested. It is then archived.
- Your personal backup folder is kept for 30 days, then deleted.
- Laptops are wiped and reissued, or recycled once they reach four years old.

## Contractors

Contractor accounts close automatically on their end date. Managers can extend an account by opening a request before that date.

HR handles questions on final pay and contracts.
