# MFA Enrolment Guide

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for every Northgate account. New starters must enrol within five business days of their start date. After that, sign-in to company systems is blocked until enrolment is complete.

## What you need

- Your laptop, signed in with your temporary password
- A smartphone, company or personal, with the NorthKey Authenticator app

NorthKey only stores a code generator. It cannot read your phone's data.

## Enrol in five steps

1. Install NorthKey Authenticator from your phone's app store.
2. On your laptop, go to security.northgate.example and sign in.
3. Choose Add Method, then Authenticator App. A QR code appears.
4. In NorthKey, tap the plus icon and scan the code.
5. Approve the test request on your phone.

## Backup method

Add a second method on the same page. We recommend a hardware security key, which you can collect from your local IT desk. Administrators must use a hardware key as their main method.

If you only have a smartphone, generate backup codes and store them somewhere safe, away from your laptop.

## New or lost phone

If you still have your old phone, add the new one on the security page before you remove the old one.

If your phone is lost or stolen, phone the help desk on extension 4357 right away. Company phones must also be reported under the Mobile Devices guide. The engineer will verify your identity and clear your old method so you can enrol again.

## Unexpected requests

If you receive an approval request you did not trigger, press Deny and report it to the help desk. Someone may have your password. Change it straight away.
