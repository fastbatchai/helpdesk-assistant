# IT Onboarding for New Starters

This guide covers what a manager needs to request and what a new starter should do in their first week.

## For managers: before the start date

Open a New Starter request in the help-desk portal at least 10 business days before the person starts. Include:

- full name, job title, office and start date
- standard or performance laptop
- shared drives and mailboxes they need
- whether they need a company phone

HR must have created the employee record first, or the request will be rejected. Contractors need an end date, and their account closes automatically on that date.

## For new starters: day one

Your laptop will be waiting at your local IT desk, or delivered to your home if you are remote. Your manager gets a temporary password for you, valid for 24 hours.

1. Sign in to the laptop on an office network, or follow the delivered instructions.
2. Change your password. It must be at least 14 characters.
3. Enrol in MFA using the MFA Enrolment Guide.
4. Open Outlook and Teams to check that they work.

## First week

- Finish MFA enrolment within five business days, or sign-in will be blocked.
- Complete the security awareness course. It takes about 30 minutes.
- Set up the VPN if you work outside an office.
- Add the NG-FollowMe printer if you need to print.
- Enrol your phone if you want work email on it.

## Access

New starters get the access their manager listed in the request. Further access goes through an Access Request, and requires approval from the data owner. Nobody gets admin rights by default.

## Getting help

Phone extension 4357 or open a ticket in the portal, mentioning that you are new, and we will prioritise your request during your first week.
