# Travel and Roaming

Tell us before you travel. A little preparation prevents most problems abroad.

## Before you go

Open a Travel Notice in the help-desk portal at least five business days before departure. Give the countries and dates. This matters for three reasons:

- Sign-ins from unexpected countries may be blocked, or may lock your account.
- Roaming can be switched on for your company phone.
- Some countries need a loan laptop.

## High-risk countries

IT Security keeps a list of high-risk countries, shown on the Travel Notice form. When visiting one, you must take a clean loan laptop and phone rather than your own. Allow 10 business days to arrange them. Hand them back when you return. Do not connect them to the company network until they have been checked.

## VPN abroad

Use NorthLink as normal on any public or hotel network. The single gateway address routes you to the nearest location. Some countries restrict VPNs, and some hotel networks block them. If you see NGL-530 or cannot connect, try your phone as a hotspot. See VPN Troubleshooting for error codes.

## Phone and data

Company phones in the EU and UK roam at no extra charge. Outside those regions, roaming can be expensive and must be switched on via your Travel Notice. Download maps and documents before you go, and use Wi-Fi for calls where you can.

Personal phones are not covered, and roaming charges on them cannot be claimed back unless your manager agrees.

## While you are away

Keep your laptop with you, not in checked bags, and do not leave it in a car or a hotel room safe longer than necessary. Avoid public USB charging points. Report any loss right away on extension 4357, and remember the help-desk hours are in CET. Only P1 incidents are handled outside those hours.

Restricted data needs written approval to leave the country. See the Data Classification Policy.
