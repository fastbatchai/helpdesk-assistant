# Mobile Devices

You can read company email and Teams on a phone or tablet once it is enrolled in NorthMDM, our device management system. This applies to both company and personal devices.

## Company phones

Field, warehouse-lead and on-call staff, along with some managers, get a company phone. Your manager requests one through the portal. Phones are replaced every three years. Reasonable personal use is allowed, but the usage may be visible on the bill.

## Using your own phone

A personal phone may be used if it runs a supported version of iOS or Android, released within the last three years. To enrol:

1. Install NorthMDM from your app store.
2. Sign in with your Northgate account and approve the MFA request.
3. Accept the profile. Company apps are installed in a separate work area.

## What IT can and cannot see

On personal phones, IT can see the model, the operating system version and whether a screen lock is on. IT can remove the work area. IT cannot see your photos, messages, browsing or location.

Company phones can be located if they are reported lost.

## Requirements

- A screen lock with six digits or biometrics
- The latest operating system updates within 30 days of release
- No jailbreak or rooting

A phone that falls out of compliance loses access to company apps until the issue is fixed.

## Lost or stolen devices

Phone the help desk on extension 4357 right away, using any phone. We will wipe a company phone completely. On a personal phone we only remove the work area. If it held your NorthKey Authenticator, see the MFA Enrolment Guide.

## Leaving Northgate

When you leave, company phones go back along with your laptop. The work area on a personal phone is removed automatically on your last day.
