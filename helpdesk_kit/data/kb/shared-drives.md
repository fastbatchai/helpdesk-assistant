# Shared Drives

Shared drives hold team files that more than one person needs. Each department has its own area on the N: drive. You need to be in an office or on the VPN to reach them.

## Connect to a drive

Company laptops map the N: drive automatically at sign-in. If yours is missing, connect to the VPN, open File Explorer and enter \\files.northgate.example\shared in the address bar, then restart your laptop.

## Getting access

Every folder has a data owner, generally a team manager. To get access, open an Access Request in the portal and name the folder. The data owner receives the request and approves it, normally within two business days. The help desk cannot give you access without their approval.

## What to store where

- **N: drive:** team documents and anything others may need
- **Personal backup folder:** your own working files
- **Laptop desktop:** nothing important, because it may not be backed up

Label files according to the Data Classification policy. Restricted data needs its own encrypted folder; ask IT Security to create one.

## Limits and housekeeping

Each department has a storage quota, and data owners get a warning at 90% capacity. A single file cannot be larger than 20 GB. Files untouched for three years move to an archive, where they stay available on request.

## Sharing with people outside Northgate

Do not email a shared drive link to outside contacts, because it will not work for them. Use Secure Share in the portal, which creates a link that expires after 30 days. Only Public and Internal data may be shared this way without approval from the data owner.

## Deleted files

Deleted a file by mistake? Previous versions of files can be restored for 30 days. See Backup and Restore for the steps.
