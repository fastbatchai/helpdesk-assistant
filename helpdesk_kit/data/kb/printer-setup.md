# Printer Setup

Northgate offices use FollowMe printing. You send your document to one shared print queue and collect it from any office printer by tapping your badge. This means nothing sits in a tray where someone else can pick it up.

## Add the printer

On a company laptop connected to an office network or the VPN:

1. Open Settings, then Printers and Scanners.
2. Choose Add Device and select NG-FollowMe.
3. Choose NG-FollowMe as your default printer if you print often.

If NG-FollowMe does not appear, install the "FollowMe Print" package from Company Apps and try again.

## Print and collect

1. Print as usual, to NG-FollowMe.
2. Walk to any Northgate printer, in any office.
3. Tap your badge on the reader. The first time, you may be asked to sign in once to link your badge.
4. Choose your documents on the screen and press Print.

Documents wait for 48 hours and are then deleted.

## Defaults

Printing defaults to double-sided, black and white. You can change this in your print dialog for a single document. Colour printing is recorded against your department's budget.

## Warehouse label printers

Label printers on the warehouse floor are not part of FollowMe. They are managed with the warehouse system. Report faults to your shift lead first, who will contact IT if necessary.

## Common problems

- **Nothing to collect:** check that you printed to NG-FollowMe and not to a printer saved from an old office.
- **Badge not recognised:** Facilities manages badges, so contact them.
- **Paper jam or no toner:** use the number on the printer's sticker to report it to Facilities.
- **Home printers:** USB printers usually work without any setup. We do not support home printers.

For anything else, open a ticket in the help-desk portal with the printer number.
