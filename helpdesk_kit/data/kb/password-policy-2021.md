# Password Policy

All staff must follow the rules below to keep Northgate systems secure.

## Password rules

- Passwords must be at least 8 characters long.
- Passwords must include an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a symbol.
- You cannot reuse any of your last 3 passwords.
- Never write your password down or share it with a colleague.

## How often you must change it

All passwords expire every 90 days. You will receive an email reminder 10 days before expiry. If you do not change your password in time, your account will be disabled until you contact the help desk.

## How to reset your password

Password resets are handled by the help desk. To request a reset:

1. Phone the help desk or email helpdesk@northgate.example from a colleague's account.
2. Give your employee number and date of birth.
3. The engineer will read you a temporary password over the phone.
4. Change the temporary password the next time you sign in.

Resets are normally completed within one business day.

## Failed sign-ins

After three failed attempts your account is locked. Only the help desk can unlock it, so please call during office hours.

## Compliance

Compliance with this policy is checked annually. Anyone who repeatedly fails to follow it may be referred to their line manager. Questions about this policy should go to the IT Security team, who review it once every year and publish changes on the intranet.
